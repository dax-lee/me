### My various music ventures and taste
When I turned 8 years old, I started writing music using a crappy Windows 7 laptop and the free version of a music software called Ableton Live 9. 
Those early experiences aren't very well documented, but you can listen to the earliest renders of my hideous creations on (this page. <- insert the link :3)
In 2021, I started to take things a bit more seriously when it came to production quality and quantity. 
My supportive parents allowed a distrobution service subscription, meaning I could now put my music online for all of the internet to (not really) enjoy!
Most of the music I put on the internet is still there as of 5/23/2025, but that's soon to change, so I'm archiving all of that music in it's highest quality.
Now, I'm experimenting with microtonal music and playing as a drummer in a local band. 
I'm gonna release my magnum opus of aggressive experimental fusion jazz in 202(6-8).

My biggest inspirations are Jack White, Neil Cicierega, Toby Fox, The Mars Volta, Tim Follin, TOOL, Kendrick Lamar, Andrew Huang, and Tyler, The Creator.
The instruments I play include: 
Drum set, 6 string guitar, bass guitar, orchestral percussion, piano, analog/fm synthesizer, otamatone, and I also sing backup.
